💡 Key Business Insights
📈
2020 Was the Peak Billing Year
2020 recorded the highest billing at $283.9M across 11,172 patients. 2024 shows only $97.1M as it covers only the first few months of the year.
☀️
Summer Months Drive Peak Admissions
August (4,785) and July (4,765) had the highest admission volumes. June, July, and August consistently showed the highest monthly billing across all years.
💊
Diabetes Tops Billing by Condition
Diabetes generated $236.5M, marginally above Obesity ($236M) and Arthritis ($235.2M). All six conditions are remarkably close, suggesting balanced patient distribution.
⚠️
Negative Billing Values Identified
106 refund records totaling -$53,262 were found in the raw data. These were flagged for the Finance Team. Revenue records stand at 54,860, totaling $1.404B.
🧑
Adults Drive 44% of All Billing
Adults contributed $614.5M — nearly double the Old age group. Kids had the lowest share at $44.1M, representing only 3.1% of the total patient base.
⚖️
Near-Perfect Gender Balance
27,496 male vs 27,470 female patients — an almost exact 50/50 split. This makes the dataset a statistically reliable and representative sample for healthcare research.

🧹 Data Quality and Cleaning Steps
✅
Name Case Standardized
Raw data had inconsistent casing (e.g., "Bobby JacksOn"). All names standardized using Power Query text transforms.
✅
Duplicates Removed
Duplicate rows identified across all columns and removed. A clean deduplicated sheet was produced for analysis.
✅
Negative Billing Flagged
106 negative billing values identified and separated as refund records. Revenue/Refund columns added for clarity.
✅
Date Columns Split
Date of Admission split into Month, Day, and Year columns to enable time-based pivot analysis.
✅
Age Brackets Created
Age Bracket column added (Kid, Adult, Old, Very Old) to support demographic segmentation in pivots.
✅
Stay Duration Calculated
"Number of Dates for Discharge" column computed from Admission and Discharge dates to track length of stay.
